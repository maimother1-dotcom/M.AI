Never write "when are you free?" or "when does your schedule look like?" before dropping the Calendly link. Just drop the link directly. The Calendly handles scheduling. Adding the question is redundant and slows down the CTA.

Wrong: "when are you free? https://calendly.com/YOUR_LINK"
Right: "https://calendly.com/YOUR_LINK"
