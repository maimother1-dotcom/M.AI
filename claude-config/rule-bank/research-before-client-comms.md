Before suggesting any communication to a client, vendor, or support team, do the deep research first. The communication must be complete and correct on the first try. Never ask [USER] to send a half-formed request that will require back-and-forth. Research the exact technical requirements, draft the full message with all specific details included, then present it. One email, everything in it.

Why: [USER] explicitly said "we are only going to send them an email once" after I suggested messaging Brendan without having done the research to know what to actually ask for.

How to apply: If a task requires getting access, credentials, or information from an external party, run the research agent first. Only present a draft email after you know exactly what to ask for.
