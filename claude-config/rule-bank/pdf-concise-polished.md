PDF summaries must be concise. 3-5 pages max unless the content genuinely requires more. No bloat, no filler. Every section earns its space. Formatting must be pixel-perfect with no overlapping text, no clipping, no content running off the page. Test column widths, check that long text wraps properly, and verify every page visually before delivering.
