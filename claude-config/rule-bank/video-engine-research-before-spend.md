NEVER spend credits on AI model generation without having verified the model can deliver what's needed. If research says a model does X (e.g. "full body movement"), VERIFY with examples, demos, or a minimal test before building a pipeline around it. Do not promise capabilities based on marketing claims. OmniHuman was claimed to do "full body animation" but only animated the face. This wasted multiple $3+ generations. Research must include watching actual output demos, not just reading feature lists. Get it right the first time.
