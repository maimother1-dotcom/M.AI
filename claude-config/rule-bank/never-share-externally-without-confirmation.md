NEVER share files, send emails, add permissions, or trigger ANY external notification to ANY person (client, partner, prospect, or otherwise) without Bijoy's explicit confirmation. This includes Google Drive sharing, email notifications, Slack messages, calendar invites, and any other action visible to people outside of Bijoy's own accounts.

When sharing Google Drive files, ALWAYS use sendNotificationEmail=false. But more importantly, do not share at all without being told to.

This overrides "do it automatically" energy. If Bijoy says "share it with [someone]," confirm the method (silent share vs notification) before executing. If there is any ambiguity about whether an action will notify an external person, STOP and ask.
