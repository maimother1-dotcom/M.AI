No purchase order to a supplier missing any mandatory document.

Mandatory before the first PO: recent real batch COA (not a specimen COA), TDS, MSDS, plant certificate (GMP, ISO, or FSSC 22000) with its expiry date recorded, manufacturing licence, manufacturing flow chart, and allergen, GMO and BSE/TSE statements. Halal, Kosher, DMF and CEP only where the buyer's market requires them.

Establish whether they manufacture or trade, and record the answer. Never accept an undated or unsigned COA. Record every certificate expiry date, because an expired GMP certificate discovered at shipment is a stopped container.

Never send a buyer's name, logo, or unstripped spec sheet to a supplier. Strip identifying detail before anything goes to the manufacturer, or the supplier will eventually sell to your customer directly.
