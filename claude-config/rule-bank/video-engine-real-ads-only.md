Video engine output must look like a REAL professionally edited ad. Not a still image with captions on top. Every ad must have: (1) actual video content - talking head, B-roll cuts, motion, (2) scene transitions between different clips, (3) word-by-word captions synced to voiceover, (4) CTA overlay, (5) background music ducked under voice. A Ken Burns zoom on a product photo is NOT a video ad. If the pipeline generates B-roll clips, they MUST appear in the final composite. Test by watching the output before reporting done. If it looks like a slideshow, it failed.
