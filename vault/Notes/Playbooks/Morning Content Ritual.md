## Step 1 — Find today's actual hot topic

Not yesterday's. Not evergreen filler. What is genuinely moving today, IST.

Search per topic. Two independent sources minimum before treating a story as real (`research-dual-search` in the rule bank).

**Indian finance:** RBI announcements, Sensex/Nifty moves with a cause, budget and policy, banking and NBFC news, rupee moves, big earnings, IPOs.

**International finance:** Fed and ECB decisions, US and EU market moves, oil and commodities, currency shifts, major M&A, sovereign debt.

**Indian pharma:** CDSCO and USFDA actions, approvals and rejections, plant inspections and observations, patent rulings, pharma earnings, export and pricing policy, big M&A.

**Hard rules:**
- Never invent a story, a number, or a quote.
- Never recycle a topic used in the last 7 days. Check the last week of daily notes first.
- If nothing is genuinely hot in a category today, say so and pitch the closest real alternative. Do not manufacture news.

---

## Step 2 — Write the post

LinkedIn-native. Not a press release, not a blog post.

**Structure:**
1. **Hook** — one line, stops the scroll. A number, a tension, or a consequence.
2. **Context** — 2 to 3 short lines. What happened, plainly.
3. **The point of view** — Bijoy's actual take. This is the part that makes it worth reading. Not neutral summary.
4. **Why it matters** — the second-order effect most people will miss.
5. **Close** — a question or a short forward-looking line.

**Format rules:**
- 150 to 250 words. Long enough to say something, short enough to finish.
- Short paragraphs, 1 to 2 lines each. Whitespace does the work on LinkedIn.
- **No em dashes.** Hard rule across all external communication.
- No hashtag spam. Three to five relevant tags maximum, at the end.
- No "In today's fast-paced world", no "Thoughts?" as the only close, no LLM throat-clearing.
- Voice: short and direct, matching how Bijoy writes. See the `voice-model` skill.

## Step 3 — The image

One strong image per post. It carries the impression before a word is read.

- Clean, professional, readable as a thumbnail on a phone.
- Data-led when the story has a number worth showing: a clean chart beats generic stock art every time.
- No stock-photo clichés — no handshakes, no arrows over skylines, no faceless suits.
- Text on the image, if any: under 8 words.
- Correct aspect ratio for LinkedIn single image: 1200 x 627, or 1080 x 1080 for square.

## Step 4 — Cite the source

Every post ships with its sources listed underneath, so Bijoy can verify before posting.

```
Sources:
- [Headline] — [Publication], [URL]
- [Headline] — [Publication], [URL]
```

If a claim cannot be sourced, cut the claim.
