# Document Checklist

Documents are the whole basis of trust in this trade. You are buying from a plant
you cannot visit and selling to a buyer whose QA will audit you.

---

## From the supplier: before the first PO

| Document | Check | Mandatory |
|---|---|---|
| COA, recent real batch | Dated, signed, batch number present. **Not a specimen COA** | Yes |
| TDS | Contains the methods the COA omits | Yes |
| MSDS / SDS | Needed for freight and by your buyer | Yes |
| Plant certificate | GMP, ISO 9001, FSSC 22000 or equivalent. **Record expiry** | Yes |
| Manufacturing licence | Confirms they make it, not trade it | Yes |
| Manufacturing flow chart | Tells you which residual solvents to expect | Yes |
| Allergen statement | Buyer QA asks every time | Yes |
| GMO / Non-GMO statement | Nutraceutical buyers ask every time | Yes |
| BSE / TSE statement | Standard requirement | Yes |
| Company registration / business licence | Confirms the entity exists | Yes |
| Halal certificate | Only if the destination market needs it | Conditional |
| Kosher certificate | Only if the destination market needs it | Conditional |
| DMF / CEP | Regulated pharma routes only. Often absent. **Never assume** | Conditional |
| Stability data | Anything with a shelf-life claim | Conditional |
| Heavy metals / elemental impurities report | Where the buyer specifies elemental | Conditional |
| Residual solvent report | Where the process uses solvents | Conditional |

**Record every certificate's expiry date.** An expired GMP certificate found at
shipment time is a stopped container and an angry buyer.

**Manufacturer or trader?** Ask directly, and check the business licence scope.
Buying through a trader is not automatically wrong, but price, traceability, and
your ability to resolve a complaint all change.

---

## With every shipment

| Document | Notes |
|---|---|
| Commercial invoice | Must match the PI and the packing list exactly |
| Packing list | Neutralised on a direct shipment |
| Bill of lading / airway bill | Check consignee and notify party carefully |
| Certificate of origin | Often needed for duty preference |
| Batch COA | **Obtained before shipment, not with the goods** |
| MSDS | Travels with the goods |
| Insurance certificate | Where you carry the risk under the incoterm |
| Fumigation certificate | Where wooden packing is used |
| Phytosanitary certificate | Where the destination requires it |
| Import permit / licence | Where the destination requires it |

**Cross-check every document against every other one.** Names, quantities, weights,
and numbers must agree across all of them. One inconsistent document holds a
consignment at the port, and demurrage runs daily.

---

## To your buyer, with delivery

- Batch COA
- MSDS
- Invoice and packing list
- Certificate of origin, if they need it
- The supporting statements they asked for: allergen, GMO, BSE/TSE, Halal, Kosher

Deliver the documents **with** the goods, not after. A buyer whose QA is waiting on
paperwork cannot release the material into production, and the delay is recorded
against you, not the manufacturer.

---

## Traceability

Whatever the fulfilment mode, the buyer must be able to trace their pack back to a
manufacturer's batch number.

- **Direct shipment**: the chain is intact by default
- **White label**: record your label batch against the manufacturer batch
- **Repack**: record every output pack against the input batch. A repack with no
  batch link has destroyed the traceability you are being paid to provide

## Your own compliance

Depends on your entity and where you ship. Work through this with your accountant
and record the outcome in
[[Notes/Business/Trading/Reference/Company Details]]:

- Import Export Code
- GST registration and the export documentation route you use
- FSSAI registration, if you handle food or nutraceutical grade in India
- CDSCO position for any pharmaceutical grade material you import
- Whether any material you handle is restricted or notified at either end
- Product liability insurance
- Marine cargo insurance

**This list is a prompt to check, not advice.** Confirm every line with a
professional before you ship. It is your name on the import.
