# Company Details

Single source for everything that appears on a quotation, proforma invoice, or
commercial invoice. Fill it once, and every document stays consistent.

`<<COMPANY>>` is used as the placeholder in templates until the name is set here.

## To confirm with an accountant before the first shipment

Not advice. A list to work through and record the answers here.

- [ ] Entity type and registration complete
- [ ] IEC issued and AD code registered with the bank
- [ ] GST position on imports and on export sales
- [ ] FSSAI, if any material is food or nutraceutical grade
- [ ] CDSCO position for any pharmaceutical grade import
- [ ] Whether any target material is restricted or notified at either end
- [ ] Marine cargo insurance in place
- [ ] Product liability insurance in place
- [ ] Standard terms and conditions drafted for the back of the quotation
