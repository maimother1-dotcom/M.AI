## Scoring guide

| Criterion | 5 | 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Quality against spec | Every batch matched first time | Repeated out-of-spec |
| Documents | Complete, dated, valid, sent without chasing | Chased repeatedly, specimen COAs |
| Responsiveness | Same day, answers the question asked | Days late, answers a different question |
| Price | Consistently competitive, holds validity | Moves after the quote |
| Lead time | Delivered on or before promise | Repeatedly late without notice |

A supplier who answers a technical question with a brochure is telling you
something. Score it.
