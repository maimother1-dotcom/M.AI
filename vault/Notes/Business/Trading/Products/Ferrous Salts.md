## Buyer applications

Haematinics, fortification premixes, supplements.

Family file covering fumarate, sulphate, gluconate, bisglycinate, pyrophosphate. **Split per salt on first real enquiry.**

**Ferrous versus ferric matters.** Oxidation during storage converts ferrous to ferric and changes bioavailability. Some buyer specs limit ferric content directly. Storage and packing conditions are part of the supply agreement, not an afterthought.
