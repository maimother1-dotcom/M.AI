## Buyer applications

Supplements, lozenges, ORS, premixes.

Family file covering the salts buyers ask for: gluconate, citrate, picolinate, bisglycinate, sulphate, oxide, acetate. **Split this into a per-salt file as soon as a real enquiry lands**. The limits and the typical suppliers differ per salt and should not share a table.

Always confirm whether the buyer's number is the salt or elemental zinc.

Cadmium deserves specific attention on zinc salts, since the two elements occur together geologically. Expect buyers to specify it individually.
