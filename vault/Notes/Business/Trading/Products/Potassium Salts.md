## Buyer applications

ORS, electrolyte formulations, supplements, salt substitutes.

Family file covering chloride, citrate, bicarbonate, gluconate. **Split per salt on first real enquiry.**

Sodium content is often specified as a limit on potassium salts, because the buyer's application is frequently about controlling sodium. It is a row that is easy to overlook and expensive to miss.
