## Buyer applications

Tablet and capsule lubricant. Used at low inclusion, and a change of source can still change a customer's tablet hardness and dissolution.

**Source declaration is mandatory here.** Vegetable versus bovine origin drives the buyer's allergen, vegan, and BSE/TSE position. Never quote without it confirmed in writing.

**Specific surface area** is what actually governs lubrication performance, and it is frequently absent from a Chinese COA. If the buyer specifies it, expect a `MISSING` row and ask early.
