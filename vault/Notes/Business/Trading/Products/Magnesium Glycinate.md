## Buyer applications

Premium supplements, sold on tolerability and absorption.

**Glycinate, bisglycinate, and buffered bisglycinate are not the same thing.** Buffered grades are blended with magnesium oxide to raise the elemental magnesium percentage, which is legitimate and must be disclosed. A supplier quoting a high elemental magnesium figure on a 'bisglycinate' may be quoting a buffered blend.

Ask directly: is it fully reacted chelate, and what is the oxide content. Get it in writing. Nutraceutical buyers whose brand claim is 'fully chelated' will test for this.
