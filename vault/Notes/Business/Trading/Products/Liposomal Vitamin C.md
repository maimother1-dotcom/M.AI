## Buyer applications

Nutraceutical brands and contract manufacturers, sold on absorption.

**Treat this as a formulated ingredient, not a raw material.** Two suppliers both offering 'liposomal vitamin C 30%' can be selling materially different products at materially different costs. The chemical name tells you almost nothing.

The questions that separate them: how was encapsulation efficiency measured, what is the free versus encapsulated ascorbate split, is the phospholipid from sunflower or soy, and what is the full carrier declaration. Without the method, an encapsulation efficiency number cannot be compared between two suppliers at all.

Soy-derived phospholipid is an allergen declaration in most markets. Sunflower usually costs more. Buyers care, and their label depends on it.
