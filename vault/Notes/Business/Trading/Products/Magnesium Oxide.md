## Buyer applications

Antacids, supplements, tablet manufacture, and as a magnesium source in premixes.

**Light versus heavy magnesium oxide are different products.** They share a chemical name and differ enormously in bulk density and behaviour in a formulation. A buyer who says 'heavy' and a supplier who ships 'light' have a dispute that no assay result will resolve. Settle it in the enquiry, and treat bulk density as a mandatory parameter here.

Reactivity or activity grade also matters to antacid buyers and is often absent from a standard COA.
