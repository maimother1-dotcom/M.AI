## Buyer applications

Supplements, effervescent formulations, laxative preparations, powder sachets.

**Salt versus elemental magnesium is the trap.** A buyer asking for 'magnesium citrate 16% Mg' and a supplier quoting 'magnesium citrate 99%' are talking about different numbers. Establish which basis the buyer's spec uses before comparing anything.

Anhydrous and nonahydrate forms exist and behave differently. Confirm which is required.
