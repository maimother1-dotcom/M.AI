## Buyer applications

Phosphate binder in renal and dialysis-adjacent formulations, and a food additive. Renal buyers work to pharmacopoeial grade and care about assay and elemental impurities. Food buyers work to FCC and often have tighter microbiology.

**Hydration state is the first question to settle.** Anhydrous and monohydrate are different materials with different assay bases, and suppliers are not always explicit about which they are quoting. Confirm before comparing a single number.
